## The problem

Flow cytometry produces large sets of cell measurements, and scientists need to explore them visually without waiting on an engineer for every question. The application is cloud-based, so the interface had to make heavy datasets usable in a browser.

## What I worked on

Over a six-month project, from October 2025 to April 2026, I built Angular interfaces for exploring cellular datasets and visualizing flow cytometry results, and connected those components to the backend APIs behind the analysis workflows. I worked with scientists and engineers to turn research needs into usable features, and resolved performance and usability issues as they surfaced.

## Handover

I wrote technical documentation, development guidelines and onboarding materials for working students, and gave them technical guidance, so the work could continue after the project ended.

This description lists only what I can confirm about my own contribution. It deliberately omits dataset sizes and performance figures, which I have no verified record of.
